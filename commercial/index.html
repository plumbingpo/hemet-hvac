Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air system has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Hemet HVAC sizes, repairs, and maintains the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and leaves staff miserable at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
