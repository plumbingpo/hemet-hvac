How San Francisco Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in San Francisco

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
