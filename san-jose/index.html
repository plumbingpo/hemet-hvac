Keeping Your San Jose HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement.
